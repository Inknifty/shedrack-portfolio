05 — Background

From Customer Service to Product Manager

My path into product management didn’t start in a strategy deck—it started on the front lines of customer service and KYC compliance operations. Having personally reviewed thousands of retail and business accounts, untangled onboarding bottlenecks, and audited complex merchant documentation, I developed an intuitive grasp of where operations break down and where customers feel friction.

In April 2025, I transitioned into product management at Moniepoint, progressing from Product Manager Intern to full-time Product Manager. Today, I own internal fraud prevention platforms, Transaction Monitoring rules, Customer Screening engines, Customer Risk Assessment models, multi-tenant RFI SDK integrations, and LASS (Loom AI Assistant)—an AI investigation assistant that helps analysts investigate customers faster and protects over ₦500M in annual exposure without introducing friction for legitimate users.

Alongside fintech infrastructure, my remote work at Zarttech designing and shipping consumer and venture platforms like Heilen and 15Wins Venture—combined with mentoring 150+ aspiring designers at Side Hustle—grounded my approach in deep user empathy, rapid prototyping, and disciplined agile execution.
